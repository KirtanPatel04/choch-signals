Famous strategies vs our plan, full year

Seven well-known futures strategies, run with their textbook settings (no tuning) on the same year (Nov 3 2025 – Nov 2 2026), on the same 5 markets as our plan. Every trade is 1 contract after commission and slippage. None shows a reliable edge. The ones that end up in dollars got there on a few silver or gold trades, or by simply being long a market that rose this year.

In dollars (1 contract)

One silver contract moves $1,000 per $1, so silver swings these dollar totals far more than the index micros.

Fair test: every market counts the same

Each trade is divided by its market's typical daily range, so a silver trade and an MES trade weigh the same. A real edge would be positive in both halves, in most markets, with a t-score above about 2 (below that, luck explains it).

What it means

  • Our plan, 1 contract: about −$5,200 over the year. October was +$789. Every one of the 5 markets lost.
  • ORB 30 + trend and Late-day momentum look positive in dollars, but only because of gold and silver. With every market weighted the same, they're flat (t-score near 0) and they lose in the index markets.
  • Overnight drift (buy the index at 3 PM, sell at the next 8:30 open) is the only one positive in all 3 index markets. It's a long-documented effect, but here it's mostly "being long while stocks rose about 9%", and its t-score of 1.4 isn't strong enough to rule out luck.
  • Making a strategy fit the past year is easy, and that's exactly what made October look so good. A real edge has to hold up on data it wasn't built on, and nothing here does.
